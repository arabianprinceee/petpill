Three taps before the visit.

  1. Open your pet, or History, and tap the report button at the top.
  2. Pick the period and the sections to include.
  3. Preview it, then share it by Mail, Messages or AirDrop, or print it.

Honest by design

A gap is shown as a gap.

  • A course that stopped or was deleted in the period shows a dash, not a made-up rate.
  • Days without a breathing reading are counted and printed, never averaged over.
  • Your threshold is labelled as yours, not as a clinical range.
  • Built to print in black and white: dashed lines and hollow markers, never colour alone.

It's your record, not a diagnosis

PetPill prints what you logged. It doesn't interpret it or give advice: that's your vet's job, and the report is there to help them do it.
