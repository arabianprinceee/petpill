Breathing rate

For a pet on heart or lung medication. Tap with each breath for 30 or 60 seconds and PetPill works out the rate, charted against the threshold you set. It never gives advice.

And the rest

The jobs, the visits, the details.

PRO
✂️

Chores

Nails, baths, the litter box, a new bed. Once or on repeat, keeping the planned dates or counting from when it was last done. Each shows up on Today on its day, with a reminder.

🏥

Vet visits

Check-ups, vaccinations, dental, surgery, grooming. Repeat schedules, reminders a week, a day or an hour before, a note after each visit, and one tap to add it to your calendar.

🪪

Pet ID card

The microchip number, one tap to copy. The birthday, with a countdown and confetti on the day. Notes for whoever looks after them.

History

Every record in one timeline. Filter by pet, dates or kind, and search by medication, pet, note or who logged it.
