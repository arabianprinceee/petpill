Questions

Are widgets free?

Widgets and the meal Live Activity come with PetPill Pro. The buttons on dose reminders and the Apple Watch app are free.

How do I add a widget?

On the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for PetPill. On the Lock Screen, touch and hold it, then tap Customize.

To show one pet, touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget.

Does logging from a widget tell everyone else?

Yes. A dose logged from a widget, a reminder or the Watch is the same as one logged in the app: it syncs to everyone who shares the pet.
