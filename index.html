⏰ Reminders

A reminder, a tap, done.

Pills, liquids, injections, chewables: set the times once and PetPill reminds you at each one.

  1. It reminds you when a dose is due, or up to an hour early if you'd rather get ready.
  2. Mark it given from the notification. Press and hold it: no unlocking, no opening the app.
  3. Everyone at home sees it. Their reminder goes quiet, so nobody gives a second dose.

Refills too: tell PetPill how many doses are in the bottle and it warns you before it runs out.

Questions

Is PetPill free?

Yes. The free version covers one pet, one medication and one vet visit, with reminders, dose history and the Apple Watch app. PetPill Pro unlocks everything else, monthly, yearly or once for life. The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

Which pets does it work for?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, hamsters, birds, fish, turtles, and any other pet you type in. With Pro you can add as many as you have.

Can my partner use PetPill for the same pet?

Yes. Share a pet with your partner or anyone you live with, and you both see the same schedule and who gave each dose. Inviting needs PetPill Pro; joining is free. A Pro subscription also covers your Apple Family Sharing group.

How household sharing works

Do I have to open the app to log a dose?

No. Mark a dose as given right from its notification, from a widget on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, or from your Apple Watch.

Where is my pet's data stored?

Your pets' records are stored in your own iCloud account and sync across your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. The privacy policy lists everything the app collects.

Does PetPill give veterinary advice?

No. PetPill reminds and records. Any threshold or target is a number you set, and the app never tells you what a reading means. Always follow your vet.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17.6 or later, and Apple Watch. PetPill speaks English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. There's no Android version.
